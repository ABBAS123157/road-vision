# RoadVision AI – Safety, Ethics & Privacy Standards

RoadVision AI is built strictly according to ethical AI principles and civil infrastructure safety mandates.

---

## 1. Privacy Protection & Non-Biometric Human Safety
- **No Facial Recognition:** RoadVision AI monitors pedestrians solely to avoid vehicular collisions, measure proximity to danger zones, and detect high-visibility worker gear (helmets and safety vests). Facial recognition, biometric tracking, identity extraction, and demographic profiling are strictly prohibited.
- **License Plate Masking:** In accordance with data privacy regulations, license plate numbers are not stored or indexed as personally identifiable information (PII).
- **Consent-Based Location:** Driver GPS tracking requires explicit client toggle consent.

---

## 2. Software Advisory Limitations
- **Pre-Start Vehicle Check:** The pre-start status (SAFE TO PROCEED / CAUTION / STOP – DANGER DETECTED) is an **advisory software warning** designed to increase driver situational awareness. Physical engine starter interlocking or vehicle brake control is outside the software-only platform scope.
- **Speed Limits & Advisory Speeds:** Verified legal speed limits from authoritative map databases are clearly distinguished from AI-recommended advisory speeds. AI advisory speeds reduce travel speed during poor weather, active potholes, or severe traffic congestion.

---

## 3. Human Engineering Review Required
- **Civil Infrastructure Decisions:** Algorithmic ranking and repair quality scoring do not represent unilateral government or municipal authorizations.
- **Repair Verification:** AI before-and-after image matching estimates surface defect reduction and visible coverage. Final sign-off on municipal road repair contracts must always be inspected and certified by an authorized Civil Engineer.

---

## 4. Cost Transparency & Free Tier Stack
- **Zero-Cost Technology Selection:** RoadVision AI runs entirely on free and open-source software (Python FastAPI, PyTorch, YOLOv8, OpenCV, React, Vite, Leaflet, Three.js, SQLite, Open-Meteo).
- **In-App Messaging vs Paid Providers:** All real-time hazard warnings, voice speech announcements, and visual banners work entirely in-app without requiring paid SMS (Twilio) or paid notification gateways.
